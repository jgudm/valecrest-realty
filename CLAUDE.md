# Valecrest Realty

Static concept site for a fictional real estate company. No build step: edit `index.html`, `styles.css` and `script.js` directly.

## Rules

- Keep the site clearly labelled as a concept. Don't add real-looking phone numbers, addresses, reviews or statistics.
- The building drawings are inline SVG symbols in `index.html`. Don't replace them with stock photos unless the licence is recorded here.
- Check every change at 375px and 1280px wide before committing.
- Keep the booking form honest: it doesn't send data until a backend exists.
